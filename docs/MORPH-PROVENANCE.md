## Free-photo gaps

Cream, verified light/dark silver subtypes, pastel, opal-pastel, phaeo variants, topaz, agate, masked, ino and red-eyed white have **no newly accepted freely licensed exact photographs** in this package. The specialist pages provide named photographs but do not grant a redistribution license. We link the source articles; we do not download, hotlink, crop or relicense their photographs. The absence of a verified free photograph is not a claim that the morph itself is rare.

The original Commons metadata used for previously shipped photographs is preserved separately. Morph captions describe visible plumage and source labels, not a verified genotype. Photo-12 is a mottled-pattern example, photo-16 is source-labelled Silver without light/dark, and photo-18 means seven days **after fledging**, not seven days after hatching.
