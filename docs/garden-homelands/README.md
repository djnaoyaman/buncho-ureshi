# Twelve fictional social gardens

Adds Java Rice Garden, Bali Rice Terraces and an imaginary Paris during distant unrest to the existing nine destinations. Names, images and music are fictional game settings, not a claim to reproduce an actual event or traditional ceremonial music.

## Sources and original assets
- Java ensemble instruments and cyclic structure: https://asia.si.edu/explore-art-culture/audio/javanese-gamelan-music/
- Balinese ensemble context: https://asia.si.edu/shimmering-sounds-from-bali-the-gamelan-ensemble-of-the-indonesian-institute-of-the-arts/
- Rice terraces and Subak: https://www.indonesia.travel/id/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/bali---jatiluwih

All three backgrounds were newly AI-generated in the existing painterly game style. No people or bird sprites are baked into scenery. No third-party photographs used. Images keep their actual source dimensions; no claim of native 1080p generation. Build creates compressed WebP delivery copies.

Music uses original note sequences and mathematical instrument synthesis, with no third-party samples or copied melodies. New Java/Bali tracks are inspired by ensemble textures, not claims of authentic scales or traditional repertoire. Paris uses original minor-mode piano, low strings and restrained percussion. Existing tracks and tuning remain unchanged. Separate C5=528Hz audition files are not part of this release.

The numerical reports record full MP3 decode at 44.1/48kHz, exact loop lengths and levels. They do not establish subjective listening quality. Browser checks exercise native AudioContext, switching, explicit mute and active output PCM. Subjective review remains open, so the assets retain trialAvailable=true and releaseReady=false.

## Save compatibility and visitors
Encounter schema v9 adds three zero-initialized visit counters after strict validation of legacy v4–v8 saves. Existing bird IDs, discoveries, relationships, currencies and regulars are preserved. No new random draws consume the breeding PRNG.

A visit-keyed shuffle bag rotates four friends on each new visit. Pure redraw, repeated selected-garden clicks, notebook views and save/reload do not reroll. Event guests choose randomly among eligible unseen species first, then returning guests, while waiting guests remain in their origin garden. Theme matching is fictional, not a statement of every visitor's real native distribution.

Both selector arrows wrap at their respective ends. Scrolling the list does not change the currently selected garden or visitor session.
